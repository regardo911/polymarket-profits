# Trade journal

From Chapter 2. One block per trade, in a spreadsheet, a notes app or this file. Fill the top half when you buy and the bottom half when the market resolves.

```
Date: [date of trade]
Market: [full market question]
Platform: [Polymarket / Kalshi]
Side: [Yes / No]
Entry Price: [price per share]
Shares: [number]
Total Cost: [$]
Thesis: [WHY you think the market is wrong, in 2-3 sentences]
Confidence: [Low / Medium / High]
---
Resolution Date: [when market resolves]
Outcome: [Win / Loss]
P&L: [$]
Thesis Review: [Was your reasoning correct? Even if you
               won, was it for the right reasons?]
Lesson: [One thing you'd do differently]
```

Spend at least two minutes on Thesis. "Because I think the Lakers will win" is not a thesis.

Done when: one entry with every top-half field filled, for a $5-10 trade you actually placed.
